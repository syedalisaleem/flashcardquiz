Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

FlashcardQuiz ("the App") is a study tool. This policy explains what data the App processes, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following entirely on your device:

  • Flashcards, quizzes, decks, and study progress — in a local SQLite database.
  • Profile information (name, class/school, profile picture) — locally.
  • Optional account credentials — if you create an account, your email address and display name are held by Firebase Authentication (Google). Passwords are managed by Firebase and never seen by the developer.

2. Data Sent for AI Generation

To create flashcards, the App sends your input to the FlashcardQuiz backend (hosted on Modal):

  • Text you paste or type, plus optional study details (class, school, city, country).
  • Images and PDF pages you upload — processed for text extraction (local OCR engine, with ocr.space as fallback; uploaded files are not retained).
  • Fetched textbook content relevant to your request.

This content is processed by Google's Gemini language model to generate your flashcards and quiz questions. Prompts and fetched content are used only to fulfill your request and are not sold or shared for advertising.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads (banner ads and optional rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect device identifiers, including the Advertising ID, to deliver and measure ads and, where applicable, personalize them.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in Google Settings → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you choose when to watch one (e.g., to earn bonus cards).
  • See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

4. In-App Purchases

The ad-free subscription, when offered, is sold through Google Play and licensed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and purchase status to verify entitlements. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Collection Summary

  • We do not collect analytics, sell data, or share personal data with the developer.
  • Third parties involved: Firebase Auth (accounts), Google AdMob (ads), Google Gemini (content generation), ocr.space (OCR fallback), RevenueCat/Google Play (purchases).

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at github.com/syedalisaleem/flashcardquiz.
