Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

FlashcardQuiz ("the App") is an open-source study tool. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Data the App Processes

The App processes the following data entirely on your device:

  • Flashcards and quizzes you create — stored locally in an SQLite database on your device.
  • PDF and image files you upload — sent to a third-party OCR service (ocr.space) solely to extract text. Files are not stored on any server.
  • Source text you paste — sent to a third-party LLM provider (currently Pollinations.ai) to generate flashcards and quiz questions. Text is not retained by the provider beyond the request.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • ocr.space — Optical Character Recognition. Images are sent for text extraction and are not stored. See ocr.space Privacy Policy.
  • Pollinations.ai — Language model API. Text prompts are processed to generate study content. See Pollinations.ai.

3. Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. There is:

  • No user accounts or registration
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No data transmission to the App developer

4. Data Storage

All flashcards, decks, and study progress are stored locally in an SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

5. Network Permissions

The App requires internet access solely to:

  • Send images to ocr.space for text extraction
  • Send text to the LLM provider for card generation
  • Connect to a self-hosted backend server (if configured)

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at github.com/syedalisaleem/flashcardquiz.
