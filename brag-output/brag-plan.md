# Brag Plan: FlashcardQuiz

## What is this app?

Upload a lecture, PDF or a photo of a textbook page and AI turns it into atomic
flashcards and exam-style MCQs whose wrong options are drawn from real student
misconceptions — then you review them with SM-2 spaced repetition and export the
whole deck to Anki in one click, free, with no account, in the browser, on
Windows, or on Android.

## The angle

Most "AI flashcard" videos show a landing page describing the product. This one
shows the product *working*: the Generate tab takes a wall of lecture notes, the
cards stack up one at a time, and then the video lands on the single claim no
other study app makes — **the wrong answers are real student mistakes**. The
whole thing is rendered in the project's own sage-and-mint palette so it could
only belong to this app.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)

The real **Generate** tab, mid-work: dense lecture text streaming into the
Source material textarea, the `Biology,Lecture_01` tag chip snapping in, and the
headline slamming over it — **"Flashcards that actually stick."** with the
project's teal→sage gradient on "actually stick". The hook ends on a cursor
pressing the teal **Generate** button. Earns the next seconds by starting inside
the tool, not in front of it.

## Key moments (the middle)

- **The card build.** Four cards land one by one into a stack with real card
  copy: *"DNA replication: Enzyme that unwinds the double helix"* (Basic),
  *"The <u>mitochondria</u> produces <u>ATP</u>"* (Cloze, highlighted), *"DNA
  Helicase"* (Answer), *"Okazaki fragments"* — while a progress bar reads
  **"Generating cards…"**. Each arrival is its own sound.
- **The misconception distractor.** The Quiz tab. One question, four options;
  three wrong ones arrive one by one, each unfurling a small *"…why it's
  wrong"* explanation, then the correct option goes green. Headline: **"Wrong
  answers come from real student mistakes."**
- **Review → export.** A card flips front-to-back, the four SM-2 grade buttons
  (1–4) appear, then the teal **Export to Anki** button emits a `.apkg` chip.

## Outro / punchline

Three platform cards slide in together — **Browser · Windows desktop · Android
APK** — under the project's own line **"Free, forever."** and a final chip
**"No account needed."** Logo lands on the last strong beat.

## User flow worth showing

1. **Entry** — paste lecture notes / upload a PDF or textbook photo into the
   Generate tab (Scene 1).
2. **Key action** — AI builds the deck live: cards stream into a stack, then a
   quiz with misconception distractors (Scenes 2 and 3).
3. **Result** — flip and grade a card with SM-2, export to Anki, get it on
   three platforms (Scenes 4 and 5).

## Audio direction

- Role: warm bed — bright, calm, confident; a study tool, not a hype reel.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (114.84 BPM)
- Music treatment: enters immediately at ~0.55 under the hook, eases up across
  Scenes 3–4, tail fade over the final ~1.0s so the last SFX rings clean.
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset
  `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`.
  Strong cues in window: **3.70s**, **5.80s**, **6.34s**, **8.96s**, **9.50s**,
  **12.65s**, **17.91s**. Lock only 1–3: 3.70s (transition into the card
  build), 9.50s (the correct answer goes green), 17.91s (platform cards).
  Beat grid for sequential arrivals: 0.52s spacing (5.80 / 6.34 / 6.86 / 7.38
  for the four cards; 8.96 / 9.50 / 10.01 for the three distractors).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle — let the background warmth and the
  hero card's soft glow breathe with music RMS; no waveform or equalizer
  graphics.
- SFX posture: moderate, motion-matched, professional restraint.
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Scene 1 — subtle key ticks as the lecture text streams in; a short click on
    the Generate press.
  - Scene 2 — a soft card-place sound per card, four arrivals on consecutive
    beats.
  - Scene 3 — one light option-snap per distractor, then a single clean
    confirmation on the green correct answer.
  - Scene 4 — a card-flip swish, then a light switch on Export.
  - Scene 5 — three light card slides, one final logo hit on 17.91s.
- Restraint rule: nothing louder than the music; no risers, no stingers over
  the correct-answer moment, no more than one "big" hit in the whole video.
